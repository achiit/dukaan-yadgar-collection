About the shop

A neighbourhood shop where the fabric speaks for itself

Yadgar Collection is a family-run textile shop on Subhash Marg, just behind Aazad Chowk. Step in and you will find fabric collections laid out along both sides, so you can see colour, weave and work in daylight before you decide.

We began with dupattas, especially the Jakhla style, and grew into blouse material, lehenga fabrics, embroidered pieces and the small things that complete an outfit: lace, borders and tassels. Our shop has served Jhabua for years, and many of our customers come back for the next wedding.

Ask for anything from the board outside: Kerala net, londi material, fancy and machine dupattas, heavy net work. If we do not have it, we will say so honestly.

  • Specialists in traditional and designer dupattas, including Jakhla styles
  • Colourful embroidered materials for festive and wedding outfits
  • Blouse material in plain, printed and fancy ranges
  • Lace, borders and tassels all in one place
  • Easy WhatsApp enquiries, with photos and prices shared on request
